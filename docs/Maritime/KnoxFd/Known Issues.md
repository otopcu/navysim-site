# KnoxFd Known Issues

Current module: **5.0.0**. This page owns Knox/Akdeniz capability limits; common runtime issues remain in [shared open work](../Shared/OpenIssues.md).

## Known Limitations

- Knox remains offline for own-ship use, with unvalidated reference handling and no authored walking surfaces or articulated weapons/shafts. Visual model availability does not establish those capabilities.
- The current model is coarse and does not provide surveyed Akdeniz dimensions or the missing detailed weapons, boats and railings. Seven observer presets do not establish reviewed deck-walking surfaces or a bridge-interior view.
- The F257 hull number is baked into the supplied artwork and does not follow later identity-field edits. Unrelated DCS normal/mantlet maps are not bound to this geometry.
- Knox has no reviewed exterior-light installation equivalent to the authored Yavuz rig.

Visual/livery checks establish the inspected model and material behavior. They do not calibrate Knox machinery/handling, add own-ship publication bindings or supply missing animation and walking data.
